## What a build is

`lib/anime/layers/protocol.js` — five stashes, not a wallpaper hex.

| stash | order | blend | when | job |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| WORLD | 0 | replace | shot | plate. Compiled at build. Redraw on shot change. |
| CAST | 1 | replace | frame | low-poly figures. Time = `floor(t*fps)/fps`. |
| FX | 2 | add-clamped | frame | energy / lettering. Glow only through `uEmit`. |
| OCCLUDE | 3 | multiply | frame | invert / halo / tear. Screen-glued. Never the hero seal. |
| GRADE | 4 | replace | frame | print / night / gold. Last. `lumaCap` 0.92. `inkLift` off `#000`. |

Poly = silhouette. Maps = frequency (freckle, twill, panel). Shader = light and name. Stock `MeshStandard` as the only light is below floor. Pocket budget: ≤ 8 draws, ≤ 12k env tris, ≥ 1 naming shader, 0 extra PBR cities. Kill = FBO / one volume hull.

Wallpaper = more hex on `voidMaterial` / Stage night. That is how we stayed at 1%.
